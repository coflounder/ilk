What this layer wants from a proposal:

  - Say which skill misled you and what you did as a result. These skills are read by
    agents acting on them without supervision, so a sentence that reads fine and acts
    badly is the failure worth reporting.
  - Wording changes are welcome and are the most common useful contribution here.
    Send the diff; the layer's content is prose and prose is improved by use.
  - If a skill was missing entirely — you needed guidance and there was none — say
    what you were trying to do. A gap is harder to notice from inside than a flaw.
