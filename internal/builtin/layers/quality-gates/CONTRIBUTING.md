What this layer wants from a proposal:

  - Name the language and the toolchain. This layer requires capabilities rather than
    knowing about any of them, and every proposal is evidence about whether that
    boundary is in the right place.
  - If a gate could not be expressed as a command, say what shape it needed instead.
    That is the interesting failure; a slow command is not.
  - Adopted by every `ilk init`, so a change lands everywhere. Argue accordingly.
