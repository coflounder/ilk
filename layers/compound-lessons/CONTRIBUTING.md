What this layer wants from a proposal:

  - Show the lesson that could not be written. This layer refuses "we will be more
    careful" as an outcome, and the interesting proposals are the ones where the
    durable change was real but did not fit the shape being demanded.
  - Say what the change actually was: a check, a skill, a deleted file, a
    conversation. If it was the last one, that is worth arguing about here.
  - A lesson requirement that produces ceremony rather than change has failed. Say
    so plainly if that is what happened.
