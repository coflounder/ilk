What this layer wants from a proposal:

  - Say what the gate was and why it was the right completion condition, or why it
    was not. The whole idea is that the loop ends on something outside the model's
    opinion; a proposal about the ceiling is usually a proposal about the gate.
  - If a loop ran to the ceiling, say what it was doing on the last iteration.
    Thrashing and slow progress need different fixes and look identical from a count.
  - Do not send a raised ceiling on its own. A ceiling that has to be raised is
    almost always a gate that is not measuring the right thing.
