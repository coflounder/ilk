What this layer wants from a proposal:

  - **A finding that was wrong, and what the record said while it was wrong.** This
    is the only evidence that matters here. The layer's whole bet is that a written
    finding is worth more than the guess it replaces, and every confidently wrong one
    is a payment against that. Send the document, the correction, and how long the
    wrong version was cited.
  - Say what `expires:` you chose and whether it was right — too long, too short, or
    never reached because the finding was corrected first. The intervals in the
    skill are guesses, and yours are data.
  - If `confidence:` did not do its job — a `low` finding relied on as though it were
    `high`, or a `high` one that should not have been — say so. The field is not
    checked by anything, on purpose; whether that is enough is the open question the
    layer was published to answer.
  - If a check fired on something legitimate, show the document. `research.sources`
    rejecting a genuine non-URL source, or `research.falsifiable` rejecting a finding
    that really cannot be falsified, are both statements about whether the grammar is
    right.
  - Say whether you worked around it by exempting, by weakening the finding, or by
    keeping the research outside the directory. The third is the one worth knowing
    about and the one least often reported.
