What this layer wants from a proposal:

  - Name the connection that went missing. This layer exists so a plan is one
    connected blueprint rather than a pile of documents; a proposal about a check is
    really a proposal about which connections are worth enforcing.
  - If a check fired on something legitimate, show the document. A false positive is
    a statement about the grammar, and the grammar is the layer.
  - Say whether you worked around it by exempting, by renaming, or by giving up. The
    third is the one worth knowing about and the one least often reported.
