What this layer wants from a proposal:

  - Say what you wanted to delete and why archiving was wrong for it. The layer's
    position is that superseded is not the same as gone; a proposal should engage
    with that rather than route around it.
  - If the citation check blocked something reasonable, show the reference. A live
    document citing an archived one is usually a real problem, and the exceptions are
    exactly what this layer needs to hear about.
  - Report archives that grew without bound. A drawer nobody opens is a different
    failure from a drawer that refuses to close.
