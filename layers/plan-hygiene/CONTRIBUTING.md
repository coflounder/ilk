What this layer wants from a proposal:

  - Give the numbers. A work-in-progress limit is a claim about attention, and
    "three was too low" needs the team size and what was actually in flight.
  - Distinguish a limit that was wrong from a limit that was right and ignored. This
    layer can only fix the first, and quietly raising a ceiling to stop a check
    failing is how a limit stops meaning anything.
  - If evidence-for-finished-work was the friction, say what evidence you had
    instead. The requirement is that completion is legible to somebody else; the
    form it takes is negotiable.
