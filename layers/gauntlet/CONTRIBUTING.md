What this layer wants from a proposal:

  - Send the bar, not the verdict. What a critic was given to compare against is the
    whole difference between a judgement and a signature, and good bars for kinds of
    work this layer has not seen — data migrations, prompts, infrastructure — are the
    most useful thing anybody can send.
  - If a critic passed something that turned out to be wrong, say what it was holding
    when it decided. Nearly always the bar described the intent rather than the
    artifact, and that is a change to the skill.
  - If `## Largest gap` started reading like a formality in your repository, say what
    it degenerated into. That check is the one thing here that cannot be satisfied
    without looking, and evidence that it can be is evidence this layer does not work.
  - Do not send a fifth section. Every one of these is paid for on every finished
    document, and the layer is one section away from being a form people fill in.
